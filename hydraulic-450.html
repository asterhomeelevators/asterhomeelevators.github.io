Overview

From intimate homes to multilevel villas.

The Hydraulic 450 lifts the cabin with a hydraulic drive and 2:1 suspension, giving a soft start, gentle stop and quiet ride between floors. It needs only a mini machine room and a shallow 250 mm pit, which keeps civil work modest.

With up to three car entrances and a choice of manual or automatic doors, it adapts to the way your floors are laid out rather than the other way round.

Ideal for

  • Bungalows and villas from G+1 to G+3
  • Homes with an existing masonry shaft
  • Layouts needing entrances on more than one side
  • Homes on single-phase household power
