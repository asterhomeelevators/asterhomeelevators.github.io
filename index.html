Questions

Before you decide.

Common questions about planning a home elevator. Anything else, our team is a call away.

Do I need a machine room?

No for most models. The MR720 and Elite 880 are machine-room-less, the PANORA 360 is shaft-free, and the Hydraulic 450 needs only a mini machine room.

How deep does the pit need to be?

From 125 mm for the PANORA 360 (none with a threshold ramp) and 250 mm for the Hydraulic 450. The MR720 and Elite 880 need 600 mm. We confirm this at your free site inspection.

Can an Aster elevator be installed outdoors?

Yes. The PANORA 360 is rated for both indoor and outdoor use.

What happens during a power cut?

Aster lifts include emergency evacuation features: the PANORA 360 has battery-operated emergency lowering, and the TR680 and Elite 880 include ARD.

Which safety standards do you follow?

Our lifts are built to European, Indian, Australian and ASME standards, with dual safety braking, overspeed protection and a fire-resistant cabin.

Can I customise the cabin?

Yes. Choose wall finishes, flooring, ceiling, lighting, mirrors, handrails and control panels in stainless steel, glass, wood veneer or marble.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes. We offer planned servicing, annual maintenance contracts and lift modernisation, backed by proactive alerts and remote diagnosis.
