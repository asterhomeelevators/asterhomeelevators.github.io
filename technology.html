Built-in intelligence

Advanced features.

01
Advanced door control system
ASTER doors are driven by a closed-loop variable-frequency motor system, so they open and close smoothly, quietly, and with consistent stability. Anti-squeeze protection keeps every passenger safe, door after door.
02
Grease-less rails
Self-lubricating guide rails remove the need for traditional lubricants. With no grease to attract dust or stain the shaft, friction and wear stay low, keeping the ride smooth, efficient, and long-lasting with minimal maintenance.
03
Enhanced fault detection & reporting
The ASTER Error Notification System flags faults early and reports them for remote diagnosis. Battery-operated emergency lowering brings the cabin safely to a landing, and the GSM telephone keeps you connected, so no one is ever left stranded.
